What is brand and what is deliberate restraint. The bullhead and the Battle Red rule are the Foundation's mark. The red rule is drawn from a background colour, so it survives when a mail client blocks images — which most do by default for an unknown sender. The bullhead is decorative and carries empty alt text, so a blocked image leaves the letter whole rather than printing placeholder words across the top.

Why headings are sentence case when the brand sets display type in caps: a slide headline is read across a room, and these are letters to somebody who runs a nonprofit, often about money. The brand's own rule is that prose is sentence case.

Not shown: award and decline notifications. Those belong to the 2026 application cycle, and decline wording is still to be written by a person.
